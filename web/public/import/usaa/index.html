How to download USAA transactions

Here's how to get your USAA transactions, as a CSV file or a statement, into UrBudget.

Get your transactions from USAA

  1. Sign in at usaa.com on a computer and open the account.
  2. Look for the export or download option with the account's transactions. Choose CSV and the dates since your last import.
  3. Or download a statement. USAA lets you download your bank statements. Save one as a PDF and let the assistant read it.

Do one account at a time: checking, savings and each card go into their own account in UrBudget. For a long history, statements work month by month. If two downloads or statements cover the same days, the review catches the repeats.

Bring it into UrBudget

For a CSV, set Dates in the file to the order it uses: 10/31/2026 is month/day/year. Import checking, savings and each card into their own account. Then choose Import file on your USAA account and check the preview; the import guide has every step, and what the assistant sends to your AI provider when it reads a statement.

If something looks wrong

  • Purchases show up as money coming in. The CSV writes them as positive amounts. Tick Purchases are positive (UrBudget 1.0.3 and later) and preview again.
  • You can't find a download. Use a statement PDF and the assistant, as above.

Budget with your USAA file

Free on Windows and macOS. File import needs no bank connection. Optional SimpleFIN bank sync is available for supported US and Canadian institutions.
